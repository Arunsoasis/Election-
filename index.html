My promise

Too many councillors disappear after they win and return four years later, just in time for the election. I will be out in the ward listening to every resident, not only when I’m asking for your vote.

What I will work on

Safe neighbourhoods

  • Stronger public and neighbourhood safety
  • A visible police presence in the ward
  • Safer roads for drivers, cyclists and pedestrians

Getting around in winter

  • Snow removal that happens on time
  • An end to winter TTC delays

Affordable living

  • Wages don’t cover rent and food for many middle-class families
  • Real attention to the housing crisis
  • Action on homelessness

A city hall that answers to you

  • Faster city response times
  • Accountability for city staff
  • Public numbers on what is budgeted and what is spent on each project
  • Residents involved in projects in their neighbourhood

About Arun

I have lived in Ward 24 for a long time. I’m a real estate agent, and I came to Canada as an immigrant, so I understand the pressure on a middle-class family trying to make ends meet.

I’m running because residents deserve a councillor who listens, responds and explains where the money goes.

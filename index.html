Arun Sivanandan has lived in Ward 24 for a long time. He came to Canada as an immigrant and built his life here the way most families in Guildwood, West Hill, Morningside, Woburn and Scarborough Village do: by working hard and stretching every paycheque.

As a real estate agent, he sits at kitchen tables every week with people who can't make rent and groceries work on today's wages. He sees young families priced out, seniors worried about staying in their homes, and neighbours sleeping rough in a city that should do better.

Arun is running because too many residents only see their councillor at election time. His promise is simple. After he wins, he keeps showing up, keeps listening, and reports back on what he did.

Winter plan

Back-to-back storms shouldn't stop Ward 24.

Since Line 3 closed, more of Scarborough depends on buses that crawl through unplowed streets. Arun's storm plan covers every stage, from the forecast to the follow-up.

48 hrs before

Get ready

Ward alert by email and social media with plow routes, parking bans and how to report missed streets.

During the storm

Clear the routes people need

Push for bus routes, school zones and transit stops to be cleared first so the commute keeps moving.

Storm after storm

No reset between storms

Ask staff to clear windrows and sidewalks before the next snowfall, not after the snowbanks stack up.

Within 7 days

Report back

Publish which streets were missed, how long 311 calls took, and what the contractor is fixing.

Accountability

You'll know where the money goes.

Residents deserve to know how much is set aside for a project, how much has been spent, and whether staff delivered. Arun will hold City Hall to that standard, starting with his own office.

Quarterly

Ward 24 report card

A plain-language update on every project in the ward: what was budgeted, what was spent, and what's late.

Monthly

311 response tracker

How long city requests in Ward 24 take to close, by type, so slow service gets noticed and fixed.

Regularly

Open ward meetings

Rotating across Guildwood, West Hill, Morningside, Woburn and Scarborough Village, in person and online.

Before any vote

Residents first

Big local decisions, from developments to road changes, go to a ward meeting before they go to council.

Every vote

How I voted, and why

A short note after each council meeting explaining Arun's votes on issues that affect Ward 24.

Questions

Ward 24 asks, Arun answers.

Which neighbourhoods are in Ward 24?

Scarborough-Guildwood includes Guildwood, West Hill, Morningside, Woburn and Scarborough Village. It runs from Highway 401 south to Lake Ontario, between roughly Markham Road and McCowan in the west and Morningside Avenue in the east.

Can a councillor really fix housing costs?

Not alone, but a councillor votes on zoning, development approvals and the city budget. Arun will back homes families can afford, push for supportive housing and shelter-to-home services, and make sure new buildings come with the transit, schools and parks to support them.

What will you do about TTC delays in winter?

Press for bus routes and stops to be cleared first, ask the TTC for Ward 24 reliability data after every storm, and bring residents' complaints to the TTC directly instead of letting them disappear.

How will I know you're doing what you promised?

A quarterly report card on spending and projects, a monthly 311 response tracker, a note on how he voted after each council meeting, and regular open ward meetings.
